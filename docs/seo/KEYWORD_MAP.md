## Intent coverage

- **Local intent:** every title/description includes "Kenya" or "Nairobi"; LocalBusiness
  schema covers Nairobi as the base with `areaServed: Kenya`.
- **Commercial/transactional intent:** category and industry pages all end in a
  "Request a Quote" CTA path, matching how this B2B site actually converts (no cart
  checkout — quote requests via form/email).
- **Informational intent:** FAQPage content on Company, Products, and each industry page
  answers real buyer questions (bulk pack sizes, hard-water formulas, training, KEBS).
- **Long-tail/low-competition:** category-specific long-tails ("hard water detergent
  Kenya", "hotel housekeeping supplies Kenya") are lower-competition than generic
  "cleaning products Kenya" and now have a dedicated, indexable landing page each —
  previously impossible since categories were JS-only filters.

## Cannibalization check

Each page targets a distinct primary keyword; no two pages compete for the same head
term. The Products hub page (`/products`) intentionally targets the broader "cleaning
products catalog Kenya" while category pages own the narrower product-type terms.
